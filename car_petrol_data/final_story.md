This dataset contains a real petrol log maintained by a car owner over 14 months. The owner recorded details such as the petrol cost, refill date, odometer reading, litres filled, fuel price, and notes for each refill. Some columns have missing values because the owner did not record them, so the exact reason for the missing data is unknown. The owner spent a total of **₹98,559** on fuel during this period, with the highest monthly spending of **₹10,009** in **March 2026**. One entry was a vehicle service record rather than a fuel refill, so it was excluded from the fuel analysis. The odometer data starts at **43,012 km** and ends at **53,742 km**, with one missing reading around **12 December 2025** that was estimated using interpolation to maintain continuity. The driving intensity shows that the car was usually driven about **25–45 km per day**, but on **2 October 2025** it reached about **211 km per day**, indicating an unusual long-distance trip compared with the other refill periods. The comparison between monthly fuel spending and monthly distance driven shows a similar trend for most months, but **May 2026** and **June 2026** are different because the car travelled more kilometres despite lower fuel spending. Finally, the odometer verification confirmed that all available odometer readings increase continuously, indicating there are no incorrect or decreasing odometer values in the dataset.
